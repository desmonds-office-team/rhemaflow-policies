Privacy Policy

Last Updated: March 25, 2026

1. Information We Collect

RhemaFlow Content Manager ("the Service") collects the following information:

  • Account Information: Email address and authentication credentials when you connect social media accounts
  • Content Data: Videos, captions, and metadata you upload for posting to TikTok
  • OAuth Tokens: Access tokens from TikTok and other platforms to post on your behalf
  • Usage Data: Information about how you use the Service, including posting history and scheduling data

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information to:

  • Provide and maintain the Service
  • Post content to your connected social media accounts
  • Schedule and manage your content
  • Improve and optimize the Service
  • Communicate with you about the Service

3. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored securely on our servers. OAuth tokens are encrypted. We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your information from unauthorized access.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell or share your personal information with third parties, except:

  • With TikTok and other platforms you connect, to post content on your behalf
  • When required by law or legal process
  • With your explicit consent

5. Third-Party Services

The Service integrates with TikTok and other social media platforms. Your use of those platforms is subject to their respective privacy policies.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your data stored in the Service
  • Delete your account and associated data
  • Disconnect social media accounts at any time
  • Request correction of inaccurate data

7. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. You can request deletion of your data at any time by disconnecting accounts or deleting your account.

8. Cookies and Tracking

The Service uses minimal cookies for authentication and session management. We do not use third-party tracking cookies.

9. Children's Privacy

The Service is not intended for users under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

10. Changes to Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify users of significant changes through the Service.

11. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how we handle your data, please contact us through the application.
